About

I'm a Full Stack Developer at Southern Folger Contracting, where I build and support three production application layers: a React web app, a React Native mobile app, and NestJS APIs backed by PostgreSQL and AWS.

Day to day I ship full stack features in TypeScript, work on offline mobile sync (SQLite, local storage, media uploads, background sync), and troubleshoot production and QA issues across frontend, mobile, backend, database, and cloud. I also support CI/CD, releases, and database migrations.

Before moving into software engineering I worked in Business Intelligence at Bombora, where I built an automated reporting system that supported a $250,000 contract. That background shapes how I build: I care about reliable data and measurable outcomes.

I'm finishing a B.S. in Management Information Systems at the University of Nevada, Reno (expected December 2026).
